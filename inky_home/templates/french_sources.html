Selection and limitations

Definitions come from Wiktionary via Kaikki, sentence pairs from Tatoeba, and frequency rankings from Lexique 3.83. Difficulty is an intermediate estimate, not CEFR certification. English gloss overlap helps select examples but cannot guarantee the intended dictionary sense.

The short usage notes are authored application rules based on dictionary metadata. They are not sourced grammar lessons.

Each card's Sources and licenses disclosure links to its dictionary entry, sentence contributors, and translations. Selected daily cards are saved locally so database updates do not change past assignments.
